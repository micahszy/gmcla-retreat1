Retreat Itinerary

Day-by-day break down of all Retreat programming

3:00 PM – Onward Security Gate Stand

Check-in Upon Arrival

Arrive at Camp Bob Waldorf security gate. Collect your cabin and room assignments before settling into your accommodations.

7:00 PM – 8:00 PM Dining Hall

Friday Night Dinner

Opening retreat meal. All attendees bussing own service. Clean-up duties by Tenor 1s.

8:00 PM – 9:00 PM Counselor Lounge

12-Step Meeting

Supportive recovery gathering open to all interested singers and members.

8:30 PM – 11:15 PM Teen Center

Party: "Ease on Down to the Ozdust Ballroom"

Kickoff party and dancing in the Teen Center! All attendees requested to assist with clean-up.

Campfire Gathering

Fire Pit at Cookout Pavilion (weather permitting). Unwind around the fire with songs and stories. Assist with tear down.

Morning Yoga

Led by Jack Inman. Meet at the outdoor recreation area for peaceful morning stretch & breathe.

8:00 AM – 9:15 AM Dining Hall

Saturday Breakfast

Hot breakfast buffet. Bussing own service. Clean-up duties by Tenor 2s.

REHEARSAL 1

First full chorus morning rehearsal session in the Music and Art Center (MAC Hall).

Lunch

Retreat FAQ & Policies

Important details regarding Camp Bob Waldorf, financial aid, FSIP volunteering, and policies.

We want everyone to attend retreat! It really is a special time where you get to really bond with your family and have a lot of fun along the way!

If you want to attend retreat but you find yourself being prohibited by cost, there may be scholarship opportunities available. Please email Micah & David at retreatcoordinators@membership.gmcla.org letting us know you are interested in a scholarship.

Scholarships are offered on a first come, first served basis and available scholarship funds are based on membership attendance levels and donations. We will do everything we can to accommodate those interested, but please reserve the scholarships to members who are genuinely in need.

If you are interested in donating to the general scholarship fund, please check out the scholarship donation option in the Chorus Connection Payments Center.

If you are interested in sponsoring a specific member, please contact that member directly and arrange this privately. Every member attending retreat must pay for their own registration through their own Chorus Connection account.

If you are an FSIP eligible member during this concert cycle that has already been approved via Memcom, you may sign up to volunteer as Retreat help and count those hours toward your FSIP! Please email Micah & David at retreatcoordinators@membership.gmcla.org to let us know that you are an FSIP eligible member who wants to help with retreat!

Full refund is available if notified by Sunday, March 1st. No refunds for no-show or cancellation after Sunday, March 1st.

If you need to cancel your registration and you are in a position to donate, we will happily apply your registration fees toward the Scholarship Fund or the General Retreat Budget upon request.

Please reach out to Micah & David at retreatcoordinators@membership.gmcla.org to request cancellations.
